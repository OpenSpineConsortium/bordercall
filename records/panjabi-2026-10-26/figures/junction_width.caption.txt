Naming against the width of the window. A, named exactly; B, named within one level; profile template on synthetic typical columns, 250 windows per width, measures end-plate width, posterior body height, canal width. Filled circles and a solid line, C7/T1; open circles and a dashed line, T12/L1. Bars are 95% Wilson intervals. The model is read off the published tables and fitted on no CT, so every number is about the tables and the generator, not about a patient.
