The thoracolumbar transitional body, two truths one level apart. The nineteenth body is drawn from its own region's shape (blend 0) toward its neighbour's (blend 1) for the three thoracolumbar phenotypes, told apart by marker: circle, a thirteenth rib-bearing body drawn thoracic; square, a rib on L1 drawn lumbar; triangle, no rib on T12. One set of calls is scored two ways: against the count from C2, which names the nineteenth body L1 whatever its shape (solid line, filled markers), and against the rib reading, which names the same column one level apart (dashed line, open markers). Profile template, 250 synthetic windows per point, measures end-plate width, posterior body height, canal width. Bars are 95% Wilson intervals. The shape cannot choose between the two conventions; only the count from C2 settles it, which is the case a full-spine corpus meets.
