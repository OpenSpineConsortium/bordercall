The vertebral column as the tables describe it. A, the column in anteroposterior projection, drawn to Panjabi's published means: each body a box of its end-plate width and posterior height, the canal width hatched over it, the disc spaces at one height throughout (5 mm) since the tables give none; the scale is millimetres from the C3 upper end-plate. B onward, each measure down the column, the mean and one population standard deviation (the printed standard error times the square root of twelve), the two junctions as dotted lines. The cervicothoracic step is large in every measure; the thoracolumbar step is small beside the spread of one level. C2 has no upper end-plate row, so the profile starts at C3. Panjabi MM et al., Spine 1991;16:861 and 888; Spine 1992;17:299. No patient data.
